Join the lab

We welcome enquiries from people who want to work on robot learning and control. There is no application form: write to us.

  • Prospective PhD students

    If your interests sit in one of our research fields, tell us which one and what you would like to find out. PhD admission is through the university's graduate programs; write to us before you apply.

    PhD in Astrophysics and Space Systems: how to apply · Global PhD Student Fellowship: how to apply · PhD programs at NYU Abu Dhabi

  • Postdoctoral researchers

    If you hold, or are completing, a doctorate in robotics, control or machine learning, tell us what you would bring and what you would build here.

  • Undergraduate researchers

    If you study at NYU Abu Dhabi and want research experience in robot learning and control, tell us what draws you to it.

What to include in your email

  1. A subject line that starts with “REACH Lab enquiry” and names the kind of position you have in mind.
  2. A few sentences on the research field that interests you most, and why.
  3. Your CV.
  4. One piece of your own work — a thesis, paper, report, project or code repository — with a link.
  5. When you could start.

Email fa2656@nyu.edu

Before you write, read a few of our recent publications: the best enquiries show that you have.
