Resources

Resources

The robots, sensors and machines REACH Lab works with.

Robots

  • Franka Research 3

    Torque-controlled collaborative arm for manipulation experiments, with a matching simulation model.

  • Clearpath Husky

    Unmanned ground vehicle for mobile manipulation.

  • NAO

    Small humanoid robot used for demonstrations.

  • Hiwonder platform

    Raspberry Pi–based robot platform used in mechatronics teaching.

Sensing and interfaces

  • OptiTrack

    Optical motion-capture system.

  • Intel RealSense

    Depth cameras for perception and fiducial-marker tracking.

  • Delsys EMG

    Surface electromyography system for myoelectric data collection.

  • Leap Motion

    Optical hand and finger tracking.

Fabrication

  • 3D printers

    Printing of moulds and fixtures for soft hands.

  • Tendon-driven actuation

    Servo motors, spools and tendons for actuating soft hands.

Compute

  • GPU workstation

    The lab's main machine for training learning-based controllers.

  • Lab server

    Remote-access server for simulation and training.
