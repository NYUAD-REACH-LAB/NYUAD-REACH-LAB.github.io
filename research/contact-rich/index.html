Research field

Compliant and contact-rich manipulation

We regulate force and impedance in tasks defined by contact. The aim is to keep the robot stable and safe while it touches an object or a person.

About this field

Most useful tasks are defined by contact rather than free motion. We regulate force and impedance for assembly, insertion, tool use and the handling of deformable objects. The aim is to keep the robot stable and safe while it touches something, or someone.
